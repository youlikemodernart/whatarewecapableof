Prepared for Unitus · Private proposal · September 2026

A partnership for the next stage of growth.

Unitus has meaningful reach, a substantial customer audience, and an increasingly connected digital operation. The next step is to give that work clear ownership, one operating rhythm, and a scope that matches the responsibility required.

The context

The work now functions as one commercial system

Over the trailing 365 days, the latest validated data shows approximately $1.37 million in GA4 purchase revenue, 758,000 website sessions, 79,000 email subscribers, and 18,000 SMS subscribers.

Supporting that ecosystem now crosses customer communication, organic acquisition, merchandising, technology, launches, reporting, and the systems connecting them.

  • Email strategy, copy, design, merchandising, segmentation, production, quality assurance, and reporting.
  • SMS strategy and lifecycle communication.
  • Technical and commercial SEO.
  • Shopify implementation and optimization.
  • Campaign and product-launch operations.
  • Platform and partner coordination.
  • Measurement and performance interpretation.
  • Sponsorship and broader operational systems support.

Our recommendation

One integrated partnership at $10,000 per month

We recommend consolidating the agreed digital growth functions into one operating partnership. This gives Unitus one coordinated partner responsible for the strategy, production, implementation, and operating rhythm established in the final scope.

The goal is clear ownership, stronger coordination, and a scope that accurately reflects the work required to support the business.

Specific growth or revenue results cannot be guaranteed. Performance depends on product demand, inventory, pricing, offers, approvals, platform conditions, media activity, and Unitus's ability to execute decisions across the business.

Working model

Shared clarity, named owners

What are we capable of? will own the strategy and execution responsibilities established in the final scope. Unitus will retain ownership of:

  • Business and financial decisions.
  • Product availability and inventory.
  • Pricing and promotional approval.
  • Brand approval and legal or compliance review.
  • Timely access to required systems and information.
  • Final approval of campaigns, launches, and material site changes.

Requests outside the approved workstreams will be scoped separately before work begins.
