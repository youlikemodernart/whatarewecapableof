Prepared for Daybreak Church | August 2026 | Private proposal

A new website for Daybreak Church.

Design and build a new Daybreak Church website as part of the current rebrand, replacing the existing ChurchCo site.

The project

One website for discovery and participation

The website will serve new visitors learning about Daybreak, its beliefs, culture, locations, and Sunday experience, as well as people already connected to Daybreak looking for events, ministries, messages, giving, and next steps.

Neww will lead the web design and translate Daybreak's new identity into a responsive digital system. What are we capable of? will lead planning, structure, development, integrations, migration, search, analytics, testing, training, and launch.

Scope of work

What the engagement includes

Discovery

Discovery and planning

  • Project kickoff with Daybreak's primary team.
  • Review of the current website, content, and workflows.
  • Audience and user-journey definition.
  • Content and page inventory.
  • Carlsbad and Meadowlark campus requirements.
  • Review of Sunday, event, ministry, message, giving, and next-step journeys.
  • Planning Center, Church Center, Overflow, video, livestream, form, analytics, and search requirements.
  • Coordination plan with Daybreak's identity designer.
  • Project schedule, review gates, and approval responsibilities.
Structure

Website structure

  • Recommended sitemap and navigation.
  • Page hierarchy and reusable content patterns.
  • Wireframes for primary page types.
  • Campus structure for Carlsbad and Meadowlark.
  • Sunday and first-visit path.
  • Events and registration path.
  • Ministries and next steps.
  • Values, beliefs, origin story, staff, messages, giving, and contact paths.
  • Mobile behavior and accessibility requirements.
Design

Web design

  • Translation of Daybreak's new visual identity into a responsive website.
  • Collaboration with Daybreak's identity designer.
  • Desktop and mobile designs for primary templates.
  • Typography, color, spacing, image, video, and motion behavior.
  • Reusable modules for events, ministries, campuses, messages, stories, calls to action, and seasonal features.
  • Editorial design direction informed by Daybreak's interest in magazines and references beyond the church category.
  • Review and revision rounds with Daybreak's designated project lead and approval group.
Build

Development

  • Responsive website implementation.
  • Editable content system for routine Daybreak updates.
  • Events connection to the existing Church Center registration path.
  • Planning Center forms and links where required.
  • Overflow giving connection.
  • Message, video, and livestream presentation using the agreed source.
  • Campus, ministry, staff, and event structures.
  • Analytics and search measurement setup.
  • Performance and accessibility checks.
Content

Content migration

  • Inventory of current public website pages.
  • Migration of agreed current content.
  • Formatting support for headings, calls to action, and web readability.
  • Redirect map for retired or changed URLs.
  • Review of missing, outdated, or duplicated content.
  • Final content placement using materials supplied or approved by Daybreak.
Search

Search research and implementation

  • Review of Daybreak's current search visibility and indexed pages.
  • Search demand research related to Daybreak, its locations, ministries, events, and relevant local needs.
  • Page-by-page search intent and keyword map.
  • Search-informed sitemap, headings, titles, descriptions, and internal links.
  • Technical SEO implementation.
  • Redirect preservation during the ChurchCo migration.
  • XML sitemap and crawler access checks.
  • Appropriate structured data for Daybreak, its locations, events, and other eligible content.
  • Google Analytics and Search Console configuration or transfer.
  • Launch measurement baseline.

Search rankings depend on competition, proximity, content, authority, Google systems, and user behavior. This scope does not guarantee a specific ranking for "churches near me" or another query.

Launch

Testing, training, and launch

  • Browser, screen-size, and device testing.
  • Form, registration, giving, media, and integration testing.
  • Staff publishing test using real Daybreak content.
  • Leadership review period.
  • Final revisions within the agreed scope.
  • Team training for routine website updates.
  • Concise operating guide.
  • Launch checklist and launch support.
  • Post-launch verification of redirects, analytics, search access, and primary site functions.
Optimize

30, 60, and 90-day optimization

  • 30 days: Review crawling, indexation, redirects, sitemap processing, analytics, and Search Console data. Identify and correct launch defects or missing measurement.
  • 60 days: Review search impressions, queries, page visibility, click-through rates, and available on-site behavior. Implement agreed improvements to titles, descriptions, internal links, and content.
  • 90 days: Compare performance with the launch baseline, review discovery patterns where the data permits, implement a final agreed optimization round, and provide recommendations for the following quarter.

Investment

Total project investment: $15,000

The investment includes discovery and planning, website structure and wireframes, web design with Neww, development and integrations, agreed content migration, search research and implementation, testing, training, launch support, and 30, 60, and 90-day optimization.

A payment schedule will be included in the final statement of work.

Daybreak responsibilities

What Daybreak provides

  • One primary project lead and a defined approval group.
  • Consolidated feedback through the project lead.
  • Timely access to ChurchCo, Planning Center, Church Center, Overflow, analytics, Search Console, domain, and related systems.
  • Final identity files and brand guidance.
  • Final or approved copy, photography, video, policies, and other content.
  • Confirmation of factual, theological, location, staff, schedule, and ministry information.

Exclusions

Outside this scope

  • Creation of Daybreak's core visual identity.
  • Original photography or video production.
  • Long-form copywriting beyond the formatting and search support described above.
  • Paid advertising, media spend, or ongoing ad management.
  • Third-party subscriptions, hosting, domains, fonts, plugins, platform fees, or transaction fees.
  • Custom software inside Planning Center, Church Center, or Overflow beyond their supported connection methods.
  • New requirements or substantial content additions introduced after scope approval.
  • Ongoing maintenance after the 90-day optimization period.

Material changes to the approved scope will be discussed before additional work begins.
