Unitus has reached a level of scale and operational complexity that requires coordinated execution across customer communication, organic search, Shopify, campaigns, launches, measurement, and platform operations.

The operating context

The role has grown beyond the current engagement

The latest validated aggregate data shows approximately $1.37 million in trailing 365-day GA4 purchase revenue, 758,000 annual sessions, 79,000 email subscribers, 18,000 SMS subscribers, and $59,000 per month in earlier platform-attributed email and SMS revenue.

What are we capable of? currently supports work that would ordinarily sit across several specialized agency relationships. The existing recurring investment of approximately $4,480 per month no longer reflects the breadth of that operating role.

Our recommendation

An integrated partnership at $10,000 per month

The recommended structure keeps strategy, production, implementation, coordination, and measurement connected under one operating relationship.

Specific growth or revenue results cannot be guaranteed. Performance depends on product demand, inventory, pricing, offers, approvals, platform conditions, media activity, and Unitus's ability to execute decisions across the business.

Why this investment

The recommendation sits inside the replacement-cost range

A reasonable replacement-cost model places email and SMS management at $3,500 to $5,500 per month, SEO at $3,000 to $5,000 per month, and strategy and operations at $1,500 to $3,000 per month.

After accounting for overlap, the combined operating value is approximately $8,000 to $12,000 per month. The recommended $10,000 partnership sits near the center of that range.
