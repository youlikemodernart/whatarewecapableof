First Instinct owner-dogfood privacy notice

Version: first-instinct.owner-dogfood.2026-08-27.v1

Effective: August 27, 2026

First Instinct is an experimental service operated by What are we capable of LLC. This notice covers the current owner-only evaluation. It does not authorize access for another participant.

What this release can do

The current capability is bounded factual research from public web sources. It can interpret a request, search the public web, fetch approved public HTTPS pages, prepare an evidence-linked brief, and return the result in the same private Telegram chat.

It cannot use private sources, make purchases, change a cart or account, send to another person, provide emergency help, or act as professional legal, medical, financial, tax, or security advice.

Information processed

First Instinct may process:

  • Telegram user, chat, message, and update identifiers;
  • messages sent to the bot and replies returned by it;
  • the invitation-bound email address, preferred name, timezone, notice consent, and verification state;
  • sanitized public-search queries, supplied or discovered public URLs, fetched public excerpts, model inputs and outputs, evidence, drafts, and results;
  • value-free operational facts such as timestamps, status, counts, version identifiers, delivery state, and error codes.

Do not send passwords, one-time codes other than the First Instinct verification code, payment details, health records, confidential client material, or information that should not be processed by the services listed below.

Services involved

  • Telegram carries messages and keeps its own cloud-chat copies under Telegram's terms and privacy policy.
  • OpenAI Codex processes bounded no-tools model prompts through the owner's existing ChatGPT subscription. The exact account training control has not been independently read back for this release.
  • DuckDuckGo HTML Search receives sanitized search queries and ordinary network metadata. First Instinct uses search only for discovery.
  • Public source websites receive direct HTTPS requests and ordinary network metadata when First Instinct fetches a page. The service sends no cookies, login credentials, or private-source access.
  • Postmark receives the invitation-bound email address and one transactional verification message containing a short-lived verification code.
  • What are we capable of LLC operates the service and may use recorded break-glass access for incident response, privacy, deletion, or infrastructure recovery. Any task-specific human access is recorded and makes that task ineligible for an autonomous-success claim.

No automatic fallback may add another model, search provider, email provider, private source, or consequence route.

Storage and security

First Instinct stores its controlled copies on an owner-controlled Mac in an owner-only SQLite database. Private task and enrollment state is encrypted. Participant keys are stored separately from application data and are excluded from managed application-data backups. Managed application-data backups are encrypted.

Deleting a participant key is designed to make retained First Instinct ciphertext and keyed fingerprints unrecoverable for that participant. This is cryptographic erasure of First Instinct-controlled material. It is not a promise of forensic erasure from storage media, Telegram, OpenAI, DuckDuckGo, Postmark, public-source servers, or unmanaged devices.

Retention during owner dogfood

This owner-only evaluation does not yet promise an automatic recurring retention schedule.

  • An unused invitation expires after seven days.
  • The verification code is short-lived and is removed from active enrollment state after successful verification.
  • The invitation-bound email is retained while the owner binding remains active.
  • Encrypted task content and encrypted managed backups may remain for the duration of owner dogfood and its recovery review.
  • Value-free operational facts may remain for evaluation and audit.

The owner may request deletion at any time. Before another participant is invited, WAWCO must approve and operationally prove a participant retention schedule, processor handling, and deletion closeout under a new notice version.

Deletion and offboarding

A deletion request first holds new work and proactive delivery. First Instinct then removes or cryptographically erases its controlled private content and enrollment key, revokes the participant binding, and reports unresolved task or provider state.

The closeout states separately what First Instinct deleted, what a processor confirmed, and what remains controlled by Telegram or another provider. First Instinct does not claim provider deletion without readback.

Request deletion or support by messaging the bot from the enrolled private chat or emailing noah@whatarewecapableof.com.

Emergency and support limit

First Instinct does not monitor continuously and cannot provide emergency response. Do not use it for urgent safety, health, legal, financial, account-security, or crisis needs. Use the appropriate emergency service or qualified professional.

Changes

A material change to processors, capability, retention, deletion, operator access, or participant scope requires a new notice version and fresh consent before the changed processing begins.
